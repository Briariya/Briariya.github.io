Sestry

A personal, non-commercial project.

Sestry is a private hobby project: a simulation of the everyday life of several fictional characters. Each character keeps her own page, reads, draws, writes and occasionally makes videos.

The app uses the YouTube Data API only for the characters' own YouTube accounts: to upload their videos (as private or unlisted), and to read their subscriptions, like videos and subscribe to channels on their behalf. It runs on one home computer and is not offered to the public.
